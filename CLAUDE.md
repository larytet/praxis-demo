## German emails

When drafting German emails, for example to Dr. Vladimir Miasnikov (the Düsseldorf GP practice this demo is for):

- Write at CEFR level **A2–B1**: short sentences, short and common words, little nesting. Use at most one simple subordinate clause per sentence (dass, wenn, die/der).
- Use the informal "du". He is family (Arkady's half-brother), so the tone is warm and relaxed, not formal.
- Keep it short. Use bullet points for questions.
- Sign as "Arkady".
